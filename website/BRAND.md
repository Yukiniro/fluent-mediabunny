# fluentbunny brand assets

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-23. Mediabunny's live homepage was the visual style reference: flat cream rabbit, dark outlines, bright pink accents, soft glow, white surfaces, and rounded actions. The rabbit design is original to fluentbunny.

Final assets are in `docs/public/`: `logo.png` (256 × 256), `favicon.png` (64 × 64), `mascot.webp` (768 × 768), and `og.png` (1200 × 630). Logo, favicon, and mascot preserve alpha. Generated images were resized and encoded for website use without redrawing them. Original generation outputs stay in the local imagegen output directory.

## Logo

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production logo mark for fluentbunny, a fluent TypeScript browser media library built on Mediabunny.
Input images: the most recent image is a screenshot of mediabunny.dev, a visual style reference only. Follow its cheerful flat cartoon illustration language, cream bunny, thick warm-black outlines and bright pink accents. Create a distinct original character, not its bunny with a butterfly.
Primary request: one very simple front-facing seated cream-colored bunny with two long ears, one upright and the other gently curled outward, a rounded head and compact body, two dot eyes and a tiny friendly smile. Inner ears are bright pink. A single pink right-facing play triangle sits on the belly. Two simple round paws and one tiny round tail complete the silhouette. The outline should remain readable at favicon scale.
Style/medium: flat 2D hand-drawn cartoon logo, bold smooth dark #282428 outlines, very few shapes, solid cream #fff3df fill, pink #f65586 accent, no shading.
Composition/framing: square canvas, one centered standalone symbol, about 85% of canvas, all ears and feet visible.
Text: none.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background with clean alpha; no butterfly, no ribbons, no props, no letters, no gradients, no 3D, no shadows, no enclosing tile, no watermark.
```

## Product mascot

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: fluentbunny homepage product mascot illustration.
Input images: Image 1 is the approved-direction fluentbunny logo. It is the character identity and illustration-style reference, not an edit target.
Primary request: draw the exact same cream bunny as Image 1 in a welcoming full-body pose, seated and waving one small front paw. Preserve the distinctive one upright ear and one softly bent ear, thick warm-black outline, dot eyes, tiny curved smile, pink cheeks, pink inner ears, and the bright pink right-facing play triangle on its belly. Keep the large head and compact rounded body.
Style/medium: clean flat 2D hand-drawn cartoon artwork, matching mediabunny.dev's approachable outlined mascot illustration style, solid cream #fff3df and bright pink #f65586 fills, smooth bold dark #282428 contours. Very subtle warm paper-like texture is acceptable, no dimensional rendering.
Composition/framing: square, entire single character visible, centered, 12% breathing room around ears and feet.
Text: none.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background, no scenery, no butterfly, no props, no lettering, no 3D, no gradients or rendered shadows, no watermark. Match the supplied character's face, ear asymmetry, body symbol, colors and line weight.
```

Asset type: finished fluentbunny Open Graph social-sharing card, landscape 1200x630 aspect ratio.
Input images: Image 1 is the fluentbunny logo and Image 2 is its waving mascot. Both are exact identity references.
